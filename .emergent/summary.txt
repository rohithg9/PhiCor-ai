The user's goal is to evolve PhiCor.ai into a Mathematical Human Body, a digital twin that quantifies all aspects of human health. The core requirement is to have a single, unified, and medically accurate view for over 1,900 diseases.

Recent efforts focused on building a comprehensive Expert Curation System. This system will allow multiple subject matter experts to log in, review AI-generated disease data (both normal baselines and disease-specific modifications), suggest edits with sources, and run experiments on virtual patients. A super admin will oversee this process, approve changes, and monitor activity. The entire system must have a robust audit trail to ensure data integrity for future clinical use. Background AI scripts must continue running to populate data for all 1900+ diseases.

    -   Experts can suggest changes to any data point, providing a source and reasoning.
